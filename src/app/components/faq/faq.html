FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Explore our comprehensive FAQ section to find answers to common queries about our Sarangi classes, services, and more. If you have additional questions, feel free to reach out to us for personalized assistance.

01

Who can join your Sarangi classes?

Our Sarangi classes are designed for individuals of all skill levels, from absolute beginners to advanced players. Whether you're new to music or have prior experience, we have classes tailored to your needs.

02

Do I need to own a Sarangi to start classes?

While having your own Sarangi can be beneficial, it's not a requirement to start our classes. We offer guidance on selecting an instrument and can provide recommendations based on your needs and budget.

03

Are online classes available?

Yes, we offer online Sarangi classes for those who prefer remote learning. Our virtual lessons provide a convenient and effective way to learn from the comfort of your own space.

03

What performance opportunities do you offer?

We provide various performance opportunities throughout the year, including recitals, showcases, and collaborative events. These platforms allow students to showcase their progress and gain valuable experience performing in front of an audience.

04

How do I schedule a consultation for your other services?

To schedule a consultation for services like concert collaborations, studio recording, or custom music creation, simply reach out to us through our contact page or give us a call. We'll be happy to discuss your needs and tailor our services to your project.
